# Roast verdict (kickoff step 2)

**Verdict: Build the wedge.**

- **Real:** the founder is the user. They have ADHD and have tried and quit many todo apps. The core job is "get me started now".
- **Wishful:** a project/sub-task hierarchy (the apps they quit already have one), generic gamification like streaks and points (novelty fades, and a broken streak brings shame), and "for all ADHD people" with no distribution plan.
- **Wedge:** a "Now" screen showing one tiny next step and a big Start button with a short timer. Projects are a name plus a quick brain-dumped list of steps. A "make it smaller" button. An instant small celebration on done. No due dates, priorities, tags, streaks, accounts or guilt. Local-only offline PWA.
- **Test:** use it daily for two weeks. If it's still being opened on day 14, plan the next slice.
