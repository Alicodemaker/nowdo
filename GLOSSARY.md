# Do Now

A todo app for people with ADHD and executive dysfunction. It exists to make *starting* easy: one tiny thing on screen, one tap to begin.

## Work

**Project**:
Something the person wants done, such as "Do my taxes". It is a name plus an ordered list of Steps.
_Avoid_: Goal, list, task

**Step**:
One tiny, concrete action inside a Project, such as "Open the tax website". Steps never contain other Steps.
_Avoid_: Task, sub-task, item, todo

**Finished**:
The state of a Project once every one of its Steps is done. It is kept apart from Projects still in progress.
_Avoid_: Archived, completed, closed

**Loose ends**:
The built-in Project that always exists. It holds Steps that belong to no other Project.
_Avoid_: Inbox, misc, uncategorised

**Focus project**:
The one Project the person has chosen to work on right now. There is only ever one.
_Avoid_: Active project, current project

**Brain dump**:
Free-form text about a Project, typed or spoken, which the app turns into Steps the person reviews before they are saved.
_Avoid_: Ramble, transcript, notes, braindump

## Starting

**Now**:
The single Step put in front of the person: the first unfinished Step of the Focus project.
_Avoid_: Next action, current task, today

**Start**:
Committing to work on the Now Step for just two minutes, after which the person chooses to keep going or finish.
_Avoid_: Pomodoro, session, sprint

**Make it smaller**:
Adding a tinier Step directly before the Now Step, so the first movement is effortless. The app offers ready-made first moves, such as "Open your PC", and the person can also type their own.
_Avoid_: Break down, split, decompose

**Not now**:
Moving the Now Step to the end of its Project's list, without judgement.
_Avoid_: Skip, snooze, postpone, defer

**Steps today**:
The count of Steps finished since local midnight. It is not a streak, and it never carries over.
_Avoid_: Streak, score, points
